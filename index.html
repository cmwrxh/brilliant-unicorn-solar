Questions people usually ask

Does this cost me anything as a homeowner or business? +

No. We're paid by the installer, either as a finder's fee or a small commission once your system is installed. Your quote and our advice are free either way.

Will I get calls from multiple installers? +

Usually just one — the installer in our network best suited to your location and system size. If you'd like a second opinion, tell us and we'll arrange it.

How do you vet installers? +

We check licensing, past installation references, and warranty terms before anyone joins the network, and we track feedback from clients we've referred over time.

I'm not sure solar makes sense for me yet — can I still ask questions? +

Yes. A large part of qualifying a lead is answering questions honestly, including telling you if solar isn't a good fit yet. Send us your bill and roof details and we'll give you a straight answer.
