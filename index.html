About Brilliant Unicorn

Building connections. Solving complex problems. Enabling businesses to scale.

Brilliant Unicorn Ltd is a Nairobi-based solutions company serving businesses across energy and technology.

We operate across two complementary areas of expertise: solar and energy solutions, and technology and fintech infrastructure. In both, our role is to identify the right solutions, bring the right parties together, and provide the expertise required to deliver reliable outcomes.

Solar & Energy Solutions

We facilitate connections between Kenyan businesses and qualified solar providers and installation partners. By understanding each business's requirements and matching them with suitable technical providers, we help simplify the process of sourcing and implementing dependable energy solutions.

Technology & Fintech Infrastructure

We provide technical consulting and infrastructure support to technology-driven businesses operating in demanding digital environments. Our work encompasses system architecture, DNS and network configuration, API and logic-flow analysis, and infrastructure optimization—helping businesses build resilient systems capable of supporting growth and real-world workloads.

Across every engagement, Brilliant Unicorn is guided by the same principle: combine the right expertise, infrastructure, and partnerships to create solutions that are dependable, scalable, and built for long-term value.
